# Expense policy (fixture)

- P-1: Every expense above 25 USD needs a receipt. A ledger line above 25 USD with no receipt is an exception.
- P-2: Meals are reimbursed up to 60 USD per person per meal. The excess is an exception.
- P-3: Alcohol is never reimbursable. A receipt that includes alcohol is an exception.
- P-4: Taxi and rideshare must state the route on the receipt.
