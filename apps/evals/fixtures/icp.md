# Ideal customer profile (fixture)

Brot sells a team of AI bots to software and operations teams.

## Fit signals (weight in brackets)
- [30] Ships software on a CI pipeline or publishes a changelog.
- [25] Between 50 and 1000 employees.
- [20] Industry is developer tools, fintech or logistics.
- [15] Is hiring for platform, DevOps or support roles.
- [10] Had contact with us in the last 30 days.

## Disqualifiers
- Fewer than 10 employees: score at most 30.
- Procurement freeze: score at most 40.

## Scoring
Score each account 0 to 100. Explain the score in one sentence that names the signals used.
